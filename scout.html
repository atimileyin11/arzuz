Register the building you already live in

You know your compound better than any agent does — what the rent really is, what works in the room, and how to walk in. Arzuz is built on tenants telling us that, and it is the fastest way to get a building in Malete onto the map for students who are still looking.

You do not have to own the building to register it. Tell us where you live, drop a pin at the gate, say what you actually pay for the session and what the place has. If a room is about to come free, say so — that reaches us before it reaches any agent.

What you get for it

  • The building is listed with a Community verified badge once we have checked it.
  • When a student moves in through what you told us, we credit you for it.
  • What you pay is stored privately and only ever shown combined with other tenants. Your name is never attached to the number.

Keen to list a property you own instead?

Landlords with vacant rooms want the property form, which asks for the rooms you have free and how you take enquiries. This page is for the building you live in.
