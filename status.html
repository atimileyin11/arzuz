Where your request has got to

Enter the reference we gave you and a contact we already hold for you — the phone number you gave us, or the email address. We send a six-digit code there, and the code is what opens your file: nobody who has not got your phone or your inbox can read it, and knowing your reference on its own is not enough.

Where should we send the code?

The code goes to the number you gave us, on WhatsApp. If WhatsApp cannot reach you and we hold an email address for you, the same code goes to that inbox as well.

What this page can and cannot tell you

  • The stage, not the file. You get where your request or listing has got to — received, someone has been in touch, a match is in play, closed — and nothing else. Not your answers, not the price of anything, not anyone's phone number.
  • Your number, or your email, is the proof. Either one we already hold for you can open this page — a code goes to it and you type that code back. Changed the number you gave us? Choose Email above and use the address you submitted with; that does not change when a SIM does. If we hold neither, or neither reaches you, message us on WhatsApp with your reference and we will sort it out by hand.
  • Nothing is stored here. Your reference is not kept in this browser and there is no session — close the page and it is gone, which is what you want on a phone you share.
  • Lost your reference? The same WhatsApp number finds your file from the name and number you gave us. A reference nobody can look up is worse than no reference at all.
