Landlords are responsible for keeping their listing accurate and for telling us when a property is no longer available.

7. Verification, and what we promise

This section creates a real obligation, so it is written precisely.

7.1 What each state means

Unverified — nobody has checked it. Information is as submitted.

Community verified — someone who lives at or has visited the property has confirmed the photographs and details. Not checked by Arzuz staff.

Inspected — an Arzuz verifier attended in person on the date shown, tested the water source, confirmed power hours with residents, and measured the distance to campus on foot.

7.2 What "Inspected" is, and is not

An inspection is a good-faith record of what a trained person observed on a specific date. It is not a warranty of future condition, not a structural or safety survey, not a legal check on ownership or title, and not a guarantee that anything will still be true when you move in.

7.3 Our promise, and its limits

If you move into a property we marked Inspected, and the water, power or security are materially not as we described:

  • an Arzuz operator re-inspects within 7 days, and it will not be the verifier who filed the original report
  • the listing is corrected publicly, with the date of correction shown
  • Arzuz Care is provided free for the remainder of your session, or if you did not purchase Care, any Care fee you paid is refunded in full

The limits. This promise applies only where the property was marked Inspected, only to water, power and security as described on the listing, only where you report it within 30 days of moving in, and only to a condition that existed at the time of inspection rather than one that arose afterwards.

It does not extend to your rent, your deposit, moving costs, or any other loss, and our total liability under this clause is limited as set out in section 11.

We do not check land title, ownership documents, or planning status, and we do not hold ourselves out as doing so.

8. Reviews

Who can review. Only a user with a verified phone number, and only about a property they have lived in or visited.

Landlords may reply publicly to any review. Landlords cannot delete reviews, and neither can they pay us to. That rule is the reason the reviews are worth reading.

We remove a review only if it is fabricated, defamatory, discloses someone's personal information, contains threats or hate speech, or is not about the property. A landlord disliking a review is not sufficient grounds.

We never disclose who wrote a review. On properties with few reviews we may hold publication until another resident has also reviewed, so the writer cannot be identified — the reviewer chooses whether to accept that.

If a landlord penalises a tenant for a review, the listing is removed and the owner is barred from Arzuz.

Your responsibility as a reviewer. Write what you experienced, honestly. You are responsible for what you write. Do not state as fact something you do not know to be true.

Our position. Reviews are the opinions of the people who wrote them, not statements by Arzuz. We host them; we do not adopt them.
