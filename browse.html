What the badges mean

Nothing appears above without a badge, and the badge is the honest answer to "how far did you check?":

  • Inspected — someone from Arzuz stood in the room. The rent, the utilities and the walking distance are all confirmed on site.
  • Community verified — a tenant living in the building told us what they pay and what actually works. The rent is real; nobody from Arzuz has been inside yet.
  • Unverified — the owner listed it and we have not checked it. Treat the price as the asking price, not the confirmed one.

How to read a listing

Prices are for one session, not per month, and they are what the owner says they are charging — Arzuz adds no agent fee on top. The walking time is measured from the pin the owner dropped at the gate rather than estimated, so two rooms on the same street can honestly show different numbers.

Contact goes through the Arzuz team. The owner's number is never published and neither is yours. Arzuz takes no rent and holds no deposit — you pay the owner directly, and only after you have seen the room. Renting costs you nothing on Arzuz; the only thing it sells is an optional Care Pass for repairs.
