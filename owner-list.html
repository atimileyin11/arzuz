List your student property on Arzuz

Listing on Arzuz is free and it stays free. You are not paying an agent's cut out of the rent you charge: we put your vacant rooms in front of students who have already told us what they can pay, and they contact you through us.

Registering a property takes about ten minutes. Have the rent you are charging this session to hand, and stand at the gate when you drop the pin — the walking distance we show students is measured from that pin, not estimated, and a measured number is what they trust.

What we ask you for

  • The property — type, street or area, and how many rooms are genuinely vacant right now.
  • A pin at the gate, so we can work out the real walk to campus.
  • How someone finds the entrance: the landmark, the gate, the stairs.
  • The rent per session, and who runs the compound day to day.
  • What the property has — water, power, security and the rest. We check these when we verify.
  • Photos, taken from outside the building. Listings with a photo get three times the enquiries.
  • Your WhatsApp number, which stays hidden until a student contacts you.

What listing gets you

  • Students who already know their budget, calling about a room that exists.
  • A free verification visit, about 30 minutes. Inspected properties get significantly more enquiries.
  • Everything you tell us is reviewed within 24 hours, and we message you on WhatsApp.
