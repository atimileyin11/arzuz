What "near campus" is worth — and how to check it

"Five minutes from campus" is the most abused sentence in student housing. It is measured by whoever is selling the room, it usually starts counting from the wrong gate, and it rarely accounts for the last 200 metres on foot. On Arzuz it is not a sentence at all: it is a number we calculate, and you can check it.

How we measure the walk

When a property is listed, the person listing it drops a pin at the gate of the compound — not at the road, not at the landmark they name out loud, at the gate. When a tenant registers the building they live in, they do the same thing. We then calculate the walking distance and time from that pin to the campus, and that is the number shown on the listing.

Two consequences worth knowing:

  • Two rooms on the same street can show different times, because their pins are at different gates. That is honest, not a rounding error.
  • The owner does not estimate it. Nobody is asked to guess how far they live from campus, which is the one number a landlord has every reason to round downwards.

Walk it before you decide

A measured distance is still only a distance. These are the things the number cannot tell you, and they are the ones students regret:

  • Which gate you will actually use. The campus gate nearest your faculty is the one that matters, and it is often not the main gate the listing is measured against.
  • The hour you will walk it. A route that is fine at 4pm is a different route at 7am, and a different one again at 9pm. Walk the one you will actually take, at the time you will actually take it.
  • The rainy season. Ask about the road in the rain. Some routes stop being walkable, and that turns a five-minute walk into a different journey entirely.
  • The last stretch on foot. Gated compounds are often entered on foot from where a vehicle stops. That part counts, and it is the part listings leave out.

Closer to the gate, or further out?

Compounds closest to a gate cost the most and fill first. Moving further out usually trades a longer walk for more space and a lower rent, and for many students that trade is obviously worth taking. There is no single right answer, which is why Arzuz shows you the measured walk on every listing and lets you decide where your own line is — rather than quietly sorting the cheapest rooms to the bottom.

If you are not sure how far you are willing to go, that is a question on the registration form. Tell us your limit and we will only send rooms inside it. Browsing shows the walk on every card if you would rather look first.

What the three badges mean

Every listing carries one, and it is the honest answer to "how far did this check go?":

  • Inspected — someone from Arzuz stood in the room. The rent, the utilities and the walking distance are confirmed on site.
  • Community verified — a tenant living in the building told us what they pay and what actually works. The rent is real; nobody from Arzuz has been inside yet.
  • Unverified — the owner listed it and we have not checked it. Treat the price as an asking price, not a confirmed one.
