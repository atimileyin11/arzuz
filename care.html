Who does the work

Your compound caretaker, or a verified local worker if yours is not on Arzuz. They are paid per job through the platform and rated by you afterwards. Their response time is public.

If a request falls outside the published cover, we decline it and tell you why.

If the inspection was wrong

Move into a property marked Inspected and find the water, power or security are materially not as we described: an operator re-inspects within 7 days (not the verifier who filed the original report), the listing is corrected publicly, and Arzuz Care is free for the remainder of your session — or any Care fee you paid is refunded in full. The full promise and its limits are in section 7 of our terms.

Care is not insurance, and it is not a substitute for the landlord's own repair obligations under your tenancy.
