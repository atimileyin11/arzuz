Your safety on Arzuz

Nobody should ask you for money before you have stood in the room. Not an inspection fee, not a holding fee, not an agent fee. If they do, you can say no and walk away — then tell us. It affects whether that listing stays on Arzuz.

View in daylight, and take someone with you. A friend, a sibling, a parent. If you have to go alone, tell us before you go and we will check in with you afterwards.

Tell someone where you are. When you tell us you are viewing a property, we can message one person you choose with the address and the time, and let them know when you confirm you are back.

If anything feels wrong, leave. You do not owe anyone an explanation, and you do not owe anyone your time because they showed you a room.

If you are under 18, we will ask for a parent or guardian's number, and we will send them everything we send you. We will not arrange a viewing for you on your own.

If you leave a review, we never tell a landlord who wrote it. On a small compound we will offer to hold your review until another resident has also reviewed, so you cannot be identified by it. If a landlord ever penalises you for a review, tell us — the property is removed and the owner is barred.
