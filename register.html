Register the room you need, near KWASU

Arzuz matches students to verified hostels, rooms and self-contains in Malete, the town outside the KWASU main campus — at the price the owner actually charges, with no agent fee on top of it.

You tell us the kind of room you want, the most you can pay for the session, how far from the campus gate you are willing to walk, and when you need it. If you are under 18 we also ask for a parent or guardian's number, and we will not arrange a viewing for you on your own — that is spelled out on your safety.

What happens after you register

  • A WhatsApp message when a room matches what you asked for, with the price, the area and the walking time to the campus gate, measured from the owner's own dropped pin.
  • Every room we send you was checked before it was listed. The badge on each one says how far that check went.
  • You pay the owner directly. Arzuz collects no rent and holds no deposit, and renting through Arzuz costs nothing — the only thing Arzuz ever sells is an optional Care Pass for repairs.

Where Arzuz is live

Malete, in Kwara State, is the first location and the only one with listings on it today. Students anywhere can register, and the numbers tell us which campus to open next.
