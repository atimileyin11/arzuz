ARZUZ — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2 October 2026

The short version

We collect your phone number, your name, and what kind of housing you are looking for. We use it to find you a room and to message you on WhatsApp when we do.

We never sell your data. We never show your phone number publicly. We never tell a landlord who wrote a review. We do not take payment from you, and we do not hold your money.

The rest of this page is the detail.

1. Who we are

Arzuz is operated from Abuja, Nigeria.

We are the data controller for the information described here.

Contact us about anything on this page at support@arzuz.com or on WhatsApp at 09024814771.

2. What we collect

2.1 If you are looking for housing

What Why
First name and surname So a landlord knows who is contacting them
Phone number To verify your account and send you matches on WhatsApp
Age, or whether you are under 18 To apply the additional protections in our safeguarding policy
Guardian's name and number, if you are under 18 So they receive everything we send you
What you are looking for — room type, budget, distance, move-in date To match you to properties
What is wrong with your current housing, if you tell us To understand the problem. Optional, and you can skip it
Reviews you write To publish, under the rules in section 6

2.2 If you list a property

Your name, phone number, whether you are the owner, caretaker or agent, and everything about the property: address, price, rooms, utilities, photos and GPS location.

2.3 If you register a building you live in

The above, plus what you actually pay. Section 6 explains how that is protected.

2.4 Automatically

Each submission records the page you sent it from, the link that brought you, and your browser's user-agent string. We use this to tell where submissions come from and to spot abuse. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this site, and we do not estimate your location from your IP address.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store bank details, card numbers, BVN, or NIN. We do not process payments at all. We do not collect data about your health, religion, ethnicity, political views, or any other special category of personal data under the NDPA.

3. Why we are allowed to use it

Under the NDPA we rely on:

Your consent — for sending you matches on WhatsApp, and for publishing your review. You can withdraw either at any time.

Performance of a contract — to provide the service you asked for when you registered a housing need or listed a property.

Legitimate interests — to verify listings, detect fraud, keep our data accurate, and protect users. We have weighed these against your rights and set out below where we limit ourselves.

Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose records by law.

4. How we use it

  • Matching you to properties, and messaging you on WhatsApp when we find one
  • Verifying that listings are real and accurately described
  • Publishing reviews and property information
  • Building an anonymous price benchmark for Malete
  • Detecting fraudulent listings and duplicate submissions
  • Contacting you about your account, a viewing, or a listing
  • Improving the service

We do not use your data to advertise to you, and we do not allow anyone else to.

5. Who sees what

5.1 What is public

Property listings, including photos, price and area. Reviews, with the reviewer's first name and last initial — or anonymously if they choose. Average ratings and the price benchmark, aggregated.

5.2 What is never public

Your phone number. A landlord only receives it when you choose to contact them. It is never displayed on the site.

Your exact location. GPS coordinates are used internally for distance calculation and verification. Publicly we show approximate area only.

Your identity as a reviewer, if you choose to publish anonymously — and never in a way that lets a landlord work it out.

What you told us you pay in rent. See section 6.

Your age, or your guardian's contact details, if you are under 18. These are never shown to a landlord, caretaker or agent.

5.3 Who we share with

Landlords and caretakers — your first name and the fact you are interested, only when you tap to contact them.

Service providers who help us run Arzuz: hosting, our database, image hosting, and messaging delivery (WhatsApp and Telegram). They process data on our instructions only and cannot use it for their own purposes.

Verified workers, where you request a repair — your first name, the property, and the problem. Nothing else.

Authorities, where we are legally required, or where there is a serious and immediate risk to someone's safety.

We never sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose. There is no version of Arzuz where that changes.

6. Rent information — a specific protection

If you tell us what you actually pay in rent, that figure is treated as sensitive.

We never publish it attributed to you. We never tell your landlord that you told us. It is only ever shown combined with figures from at least two other residents, so no individual can be identified from it.

We do this because a tenant who reports a real price can face real consequences. If you would rather we did not hold it at all, tell us and we will delete it.

The same principle applies to reviews on small compounds — see our safeguarding policy.

7. Where your data lives, and for how long

Data is stored on servers operated by our hosting providers, which may be located outside Nigeria. Where that happens we rely on the transfer conditions permitted by the NDPA and require adequate protection by contract.

Data Kept for
Housing needs (demand records) Until the session you registered for ends, then 12 months
Account details While your account is active, then 12 months
Listings While active, then 24 months
Reviews Indefinitely, unless you ask us to remove yours
Safeguarding and incident records 7 years, restricted access
Technical logs 12 months

Delete your account and we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law or for safeguarding. Reviews you wrote may remain published but are disconnected from your identity — other students rely on them.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data, subject to section 7
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time — reply STOP to any WhatsApp message and we stop
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission

Ask at support@arzuz.com. We respond within 30 days and we do not charge.

9. Keeping it safe

Access is limited to team members who need it. Safeguarding records are restricted to the safeguarding lead. Connections are encrypted. We review who has access every session.

If a breach happens that puts you at risk, we will tell you and notify the NDPC as the law requires.

10. Children

Some of our users are 16 or 17. If you are under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's contact and send them everything we send you, and we do not arrange viewings for you alone. Our safeguarding policy sets out the detail.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you on WhatsApp before it takes effect — not quietly update the page.
